History and self-development

I have a real interest in history. I find it fascinating to understand how we got to where we are, how civilisations rose and fell, the decisions and events that shaped the world. History gives you perspective, it reminds you that the present moment is just one point in a much longer story.

I enjoy learning about different eras and empires, the way societies were organised, the technology and ideas of the time, and how it all connects to the world today. There's always a thread you can trace from the past to the present if you look for it.

Alongside history I'm into self-development. I like the idea of continuously improving, getting a bit better at things over time rather than staying still. Whether it's learning a new skill, building a better habit, or sharpening how I think, I enjoy the process of growth.

I read and listen to things that make me think. I like ideas that challenge how I see the world or give me a new mental model to work with. Learning for its own sake is something I value, not just learning that has an immediate use.

The way I learn best is by making things my own, putting ideas into my own words, using analogies, and revisiting things until they properly stick. I'd rather deeply understand a few things than skim the surface of many. Discipline and consistency matter more to me than motivation.
