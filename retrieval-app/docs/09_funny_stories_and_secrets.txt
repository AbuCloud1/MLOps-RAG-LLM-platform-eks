Funny stories and secrets

Alright, since you're asking, I'll let a few things slip. Nothing scandalous, just the kind of stories that come out after a couple of drinks.

There was a gym incident I still haven't fully lived down. I loaded up a barbell trying to impress absolutely nobody, got a bit too ambitious with the plates, and ended up stuck at the bottom of a squat for a solid ten seconds while my legs quietly renegotiated their life choices. The safety bars saved me. My ego did not survive.

My biggest secret is probably that for someone who talks a big game about German engineering and car culture, I once managed to lock my own keys inside a running car. Twice. Same car, same week. I will not be taking further questions on this.

On the DevOps side, I have a classic "it was one character" story. I once brought down a whole environment because of a missing colon in a YAML file, stared at the error for forty minutes, and the actual fix took four seconds once I finally saw it. Every infrastructure engineer has this story. This one is mine.

I'm also secretly much more competitive than I let on. Give me literally any two-player game, a pub quiz, even a debate about which route is faster to get somewhere, and something switches on. I try to play it cool. I do not play it cool.

Last one: I've been known to full-on sing in the car, windows up, completely convinced I sound great, right up until I catch someone at the next set of traffic lights watching me with real concern on their face.
