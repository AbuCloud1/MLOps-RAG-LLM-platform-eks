Cars and motoring

I'm a car guy through and through. It's one of my main interests outside of tech. I love petrol cars especially, there's a character to a good petrol engine that I don't think electric cars quite capture yet. The sound, the response, the feel of it, that's a big part of the appeal for me.

I spend a fair bit of time watching car content on YouTube, reviews, builds, comparisons, all of it. I enjoy learning about the engineering behind different cars, what makes one handle better than another, the trade-offs manufacturers make. It scratches the same itch as engineering does, understanding how a complex system fits together.

I appreciate a wide range of cars, from sensible daily drivers to the properly exciting stuff. German engineering has always impressed me, the build quality and the way they drive. But I can appreciate a well sorted car from anywhere if it's done right.

I like understanding cars mechanically too, not just admiring them. Knowing how the parts work together, what goes wrong and why, how things fit and function. There's a satisfaction in understanding a machine properly rather than just driving it.

For me cars are part hobby, part fascination. It's the combination of design, engineering, and a bit of soul that keeps me interested. A good drive on the right road is hard to beat.
