My fitness and gym notes

I train regularly and the gym is a big part of my routine. I mostly focus on weightlifting and strength work, and I like the discipline of it. There's something about progressive overload that appeals to the engineer in me, tracking numbers, adding a little each week, watching progress build over time.

My sessions are usually a mix of compound lifts and some accessory work. I care about form more than ego lifting. I'd rather do a clean set than throw around weight I can't control. Squats, deadlifts, bench, the classics form the base of what I do.

Beyond lifting, I like staying active generally. I think fitness is as much about consistency and habit as it is about any single workout. Showing up on the days you don't feel like it is where the real progress happens.

I try to keep an eye on the basics too, sleep, protein, recovery. I'm not obsessive about it, but I know the training only works if the recovery is there. A good session, decent food, and enough sleep is the loop I try to keep going.

I find training is a good mental reset as well. After a day of staring at screens and solving problems, going to the gym clears my head. It's one of the few times I'm fully away from a keyboard.
