Travel and places

I enjoy travelling and seeing new places. I'm based in Manchester, which I like as a base, it's a good city with a lot going on and easy to get out from. But I always enjoy getting away and experiencing somewhere different.

I like travel that has a bit of substance to it, places with history, culture, and character rather than just a beach and nothing else. Wandering a city with an old town, good food, and a story behind it is my kind of trip. I enjoy soaking up how people live somewhere different from home.

There are plenty of places I still want to visit. I'd love to explore more of the world's historic cities, the ones with deep history and impressive architecture. Places where you can feel the layers of the past are top of my list. I'm drawn to destinations with a strong cultural identity.

I also enjoy the food side of travelling. Trying traditional dishes in the place they actually come from is one of the best parts of any trip. Food tells you a lot about a place and its culture.

For me travel is about broadening the perspective, seeing how big and varied the world is, and coming back with a few good stories. Whether it's a short trip or somewhere further afield, I always value the experience of being somewhere new.
