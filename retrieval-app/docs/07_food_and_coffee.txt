Food and coffee

I really enjoy good food, and I have a soft spot for traditional dishes done properly. There's something special about food that's been made the way it has for generations, with real flavour and care behind it. I appreciate the culture and history that comes with traditional cooking as much as the taste.

I like a wide range of cuisines and I'm always up for trying something new. But I always come back to hearty, well spiced, traditional food, the kind of meal that feels like it means something. Food that brings people together around a table is the best kind.

Coffee is a daily ritual for me. I enjoy a good cup and I don't take it for granted. There's a nice pause in the day that a proper coffee gives you, a moment to slow down and reset before getting back into things. It pairs well with getting into a focused bit of work.

I appreciate the craft side of both food and coffee, the difference that good ingredients and a bit of skill make. You can taste when something has been done with care versus rushed. That attention to quality is something I notice and value.

At the end of the day food and coffee are simple pleasures, but they're the kind that make everyday life better. A good meal and a good coffee go a long way.
