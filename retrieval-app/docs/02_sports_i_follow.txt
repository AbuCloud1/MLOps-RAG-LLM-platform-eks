Sports I follow

I follow a good range of sports and enjoy watching as much as doing. Football is the main one for me. I keep up with the Premier League and enjoy the big European nights, the Champions League especially. I like the tactical side of the game, not just the goals, watching how teams set up and adapt.

Combat sports are another big interest. I follow boxing and MMA. There's a raw skill and discipline to it that I respect, the conditioning these athletes have is on another level. Big fight nights are always worth staying up for.

I also keep an eye on basketball. The NBA has a great flow to it and the athleticism is incredible. I enjoy following the standout players and the playoff races when they heat up.

Cricket is in the mix too. It's a slower burn than the others but there's real depth to it, the strategy over a long format, the momentum swings. I enjoy following the big international matches and tournaments.

What ties it all together is that I like sport as a whole, the competition, the skill, the stories around the athletes. Whether it's ninety minutes of football or a five round fight, I appreciate people who are the best at what they do and the work it takes to get there.
